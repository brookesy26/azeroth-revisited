# Content policy

The audience is returning Vanilla/Classic players first, followed by players moving from Retail to Forever. Comparisons explain old assumptions to recheck. They do not expand the site into Retail gameplay guides.

Use official Blizzard announcements for launch dates, beta access, races, areas, class availability and announced systems. Include a direct source link and last review date. Community talent data is separately attributed and labelled beta. Do not present datamined or unfinished content as released or guaranteed.

Explain new race/class combinations separately from new classes. Unknown quest objectives, coordinates, drop rates, rotations and encounter mechanics should stay unknown until a reliable version-specific source exists. Practical preparation advice is distinguishable from confirmed game mechanics. Avoid claiming a beta build is best without testing evidence.

News imports retain source title, URL and date and link to the original. Do not copy full posts. Article scenery is atmosphere and may not illustrate a news event. Feed availability does not constitute an official Blizzard game-data API.

When syncing talents, retain build, generated date, provider, licensing and attribution. Validate ranks, point limits and prerequisite references. If the maintained provider becomes unavailable, keep the last valid snapshot with its age visible; do not silently invent new trees.

Release timing uses a timezone-qualified instant. If official pages conflict, resolve the discrepancy editorially and record the chosen source. Countdown values are a convenience, not a guarantee that servers are available.
